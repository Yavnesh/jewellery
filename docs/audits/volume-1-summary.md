# Volume 1: Executive Summary & Product Vision Audit

## What we did?
- Established the overall product vision for a luxury B2C jewelry e-commerce platform.
- Defined the target audience, user personas, and brand identity.
- Outlined the core technology stack (Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, MySQL).
- Audited the existing codebase to ensure it aligns with luxury brand guidelines.

## Why we did that?
- The existing codebase was a prototype that lacked a cohesive vision.
- To set a standard for what a "luxury" digital experience means before writing code.
- To align all future engineering and architectural decisions with the core business goals.

## What improved after the changes?
- We established a clear roadmap for all subsequent volumes.
- We defined strict visual and technical standards that ensure the final product feels premium, responsive, and secure.
